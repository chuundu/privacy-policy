Choovis Automation

Personal assistant tooling for calendar digests, event scheduling, and inbox triage.

This internal automation connects to Google Calendar and Gmail (anchoolwe@gmail.com) to generate daily executive digests, surface urgent emails, and create events/reminders on my behalf. No other users have access.

  • Calendars: read/write access for incident digests and fast scheduling.
  • Gmail: read/modify/send access for inbox triage alerts and quick replies.
  • User profile: used only to identify the authorized Google account.
